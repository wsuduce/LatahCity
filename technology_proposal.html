Enhancing Community Engagement in Latah, WA with AI Automation

A Smart, Multi-Channel Communication Platform

Presenter: Adam Murphy

Date: February 11, 2025

Introduction & Overview

Purpose: To demonstrate how AI can streamline communication and service delivery in our town.

Key Components:

  • A modern website with live news and community updates.
  • An AI chatbot for real-time text-based support.
  • A phone-based chatbot for voice interaction.
  • A live Facebook feed widget for timely updates.
  • A centralized contact information area.

The Need for AI in Small Towns

Current Challenges:

  • Limited Town Hall hours and staff availability.
  • Residents needing timely information outside of business hours.
  • Manual processes (phone calls, emails) slowing down service.

Opportunity:

  • Use AI automation to provide 24/7 support.
  • Offer multiple access points (website, text chat, and phone) to meet different resident preferences.

How Our System Works

Website & Chatbot Integration:

  • News Section: Includes the latest meeting agendas and community announcements.
  • Chat Feature: Residents can type questions on the website and get immediate responses.

Phone Integration:

  • Residents can call a dedicated number and interact with the same AI service via voice.
  • The phone chatbot mirrors the website’s functionality, ensuring consistency and reliability.

Social Media Integration:

  • A scrollable Facebook feed displays live updates and events.

Central Contact Block:

  • Town Hall’s Phone: 509-418-9676
  • Email and Physical Address: Prominently displayed on the website.

Benefits to Our Community

For Residents:

  • Instant, 24/7 access to town information.
  • Multiple channels to choose from: web chat, phone, or social media.
  • Transparency through regularly updated meeting agendas and news.

For Town Staff:

  • Reduced repetitive calls and inquiries.
  • More time to focus on in-person services and complex issues.
  • A unified dashboard that tracks common questions and resident feedback.

Implementation & Next Steps

Phased Rollout:

  • Begin with a pilot project for a few months and gather community feedback.
  • Gradually expand functionalities (e.g., adding SMS integration, refining AI responses).

Training & Support:

  • Train Town Hall staff to monitor and update the AI’s knowledge base.
  • Use analytics from both chat and phone interactions to continuously improve service.

Budget & Cost Efficiency:

  • Leverage affordable cloud-based solutions and off-the-shelf chatbot platforms.
  • Emphasize the return on investment through improved efficiency and resident satisfaction.

Conclusion & Q&A

Recap:

  • Our solution modernizes community communication by offering seamless, multi-channel AI support.
  • It provides round-the-clock service, reduces staff workload, and ensures all residents stay informed.

Call-to-Action: Request approval for a pilot project to implement this system.
